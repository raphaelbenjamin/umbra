Chinese–English offline dictionary

Primary entries are from the CC-CEDICT project and its contributors, distributed
by MDBG and compiled to SQLite by Lumen.
https://cc-cedict.org/
https://github.com/LivingTwice/lumen/releases/tag/dictionaries-zh-1

Additional Mandarin headwords, English definitions and pinyin are by English
Wiktionary contributors, extracted by Kaikki / Wiktextract on October 30, 2026
from the October 1, 2026 Wiktionary dump.
https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Chinese/
https://en.wiktionary.org/ (word entries and their revision histories)

Umbra combined the two datasets, filtered and deduplicated the additional
definitions, and normalized their lookup keys. This adapted dictionary is
distributed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
